# Tap Chat Roadmap

This is the plan for the next Tap Chat releases. It can change as we learn from users. Ideas and votes are welcome in the [GitHub issues](https://github.com/wpdevup/tap-chat/issues) and the [support forum](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/tap-chat/).

## Principles

- **Simple stays simple.** Each new option goes inside an existing screen, or needs no setting at all.
- **Off by default.** Updating never changes how your button behaves. You turn new features on when you want them.
- **Privacy first.** No cookies, no personal data, no third-party requests.
- **Built from real requests.** Priorities come from support threads and feature requests, ours and the wider community's.

---

## 2.5.0 – WooCommerce 🔜

- [ ] **Product page button.** A WhatsApp button on the product page, next to or below "Add to cart".
- [ ] **Out-of-stock products.** A dedicated message or button text, for example "Ask when it's back".
- [ ] **Variations in the message.** The selected size, colour or other attribute is added to the product message.
- [ ] **Product categories.** Hide the button on chosen product categories.

## 2.6.0 – Analytics

- [ ] **Clicks per agent.** See which team member visitors choose.
- [ ] **Click source.** Floating button, welcome bubble, agent picker, `#tapchat` links, product button or shortcode.
- [ ] **Traffic source.** Referrers and UTM campaigns, still cookieless and first-party.

## 2.7.0 – Spreading chats across the team

- [ ] **Agent order.** Random order, or online agents first.
- [ ] **Number rotation.** Rotate between several numbers in single-button mode.
